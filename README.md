# Collaborative-study-room
Real-time online study space supporting chat,white board,flashcards quizzez ,scheduling,and group productivity.


# Frontend :
  Chat UI,shared whiteboard,flashboard/quiz UI,scheduler,pomodoro timer,admin panel,session recording mock,responsive UI.

# Backend:
  Websocket server,auth,chat/whiteboard persistence,reminders backend,quiz/leaderboard API.

# Sustainable Development Goals
{(SDG4,SDG8) Quality education , Decent work & Economic Growth} /n
Sustainable Development Goal 4 is a commitment to ensuring inclusive and equitable quality education and promoting lifelong learning opportunities for all.
Sustainable Development Goal 8 is about "decent work and economic growth" and is one of the 17 Sustainable Development Goals which were established by the United Nations General Assembly in 2015.
